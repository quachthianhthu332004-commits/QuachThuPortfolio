## Business Context

### Problem Statement

- Candidate data was scattered across emails, spreadsheets, and documents, making information difficult to manage.
- Manual CV screening and data entry increased administrative effort and recruitment time.
- Recruiters lacked an efficient way to search, filter, and retrieve candidate profiles.
- Candidate status tracking was inconsistent, limiting visibility throughout the recruitment process.
- The existing workflow was difficult to scale as recruitment volume increased.

### Business Drivers

To address these challenges, the organization aimed to develop a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Applicant Tracking System that would standardize and streamline the recruitment workflow.

The primary business objectives included:

- Centralize candidate information into a single recruitment platform.
- Automate CV parsing to reduce manual data entry and improve screening efficiency.
- Enable keyword-based candidate search for faster profile retrieval and filtering.
- Standardize recruitment pipeline management by tracking candidate status throughout the hiring process.
- Improve collaboration among HR, HR Managers, Hiring Managers, and Administrators through role-based access control.
- Establish a scalable foundation for future enhancements such as AI-powered candidate ranking, email automation, reporting dashboards, and Talent Pool management.


## Solution Overview

The Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is an AI-assisted recruitment platform designed to centralize candidate management and streamline the hiring process. The system automates CV parsing, enables keyword-based candidate search, and provides a structured recruitment pipeline, allowing HR teams to manage recruitment activities more efficiently within a single platform.

As an MVP solution, the project focuses on delivering essential recruitment capabilities while establishing a scalable foundation for future enhancements.

### Core Capabilities

- Upload and manage CVs through single or bulk uploads with file validation.
- Automatically extract candidate information using AI-powered CV parsing.
- Manage candidate profiles with centralized storage and editing capabilities.
- Search and filter candidates using keywords and profile attributes.
- Track recruitment progress through a structured hiring pipeline.
- Manage user accounts and permissions with role-based access control (RBAC).

### Scope

**In Scope**

- Trip planning & itinerary (CV upload - single & bulk)
- AI-assisted CV parsing
- Candidate profile management
- Keyword search & filtering
- Recruitment pipeline management
- User account & role management
- Dashboard overview

**Out of Scope**

- Job posting platform integration
- Automated candidate email notifications
- Talent Pool management
- Advanced reporting & analytics
- AI-powered candidate ranking

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- Support single and bulk CV uploads in PDF, DOC, and DOCX formats.
- Validate uploaded CV files with a maximum file size of 5 MB before processing.
- Automatically extract at least 5 candidate attributes, including Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Years of Experience, Skills, and Applied Position.
- Detect duplicate candidates based on email or phone number before creating a new profile.
- Store original CV files on Cloud Storage while saving metadata and parsed information in the database.
- Manage candidate progression through five recruitment stages: **New → Screening → Interview → Hired → Rejected**.
- Support role-based access for HR, HR Manager, Admin, and Hiring Manager across system modules.

### Business Rules

- Only authenticated users can access the system based on assigned roles and permissions.
- Recruiters must select an active recruitment campaign before uploading a CV.
- The system only accepts PDF, DOC, and DOCX files with a maximum size of 5 MB.
- Duplicate detection is performed using the candidate's email or phone number before creating a new profile.
- Recruitment campaigns in **Closed** status cannot receive additional CV submissions.
- Candidate information extracted by AI can be reviewed and updated manually before being finalized in the system.

### Non-functional Requirements

- **Security:** Candidate information must be securely stored and accessible only to authorized users.
- **Performance:** Candidate search results must be returned within 3 seconds for MVP datasets.
- **Availability:** Core system functions must operate reliably during MVP demonstrations and testing.
- **Scalability:** The system should support future integration with Email Automation and Analytics Dashboard.
